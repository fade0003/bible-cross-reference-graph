Changes to this site, newest first

Dated notes on what has reached the live pages.

October 30, 2026 Live

The ontology is a 3D map

The Ontology page now opens as an orbitable 3D view. Lenses bring forward Van Dorn's two powers, the Angel of the LORD, the Nephilim, and the textual veil. Textual Variants can be filtered to “Van Dorn spiritual cruxes.”

  • A short disclaimer stays on screen on a phone. The map draws before the verse file loads. Spinning stops when the view is idle or you return to the tree, and it stays off when reduced motion is requested.
  • The legend labels the keyboard controls, touch can drag or pinch the map, and the badge reads “Textual veil.”
  • The sources cite Foreman and Van Dorn. The notes now separate Rahlfs's Septuagint from Codex Alexandrinus at Genesis 6:2, name Isaiah 19:1 as the cloud-rider line, record the 1QIsa-a reading of Isaiah 63:9, and place the pierced-hands fragment at Nahal Hever rather than Qumran.
  • The site has a favicon.
September 20, 2026 Live

Mobile layout, theme words, and verse-dot previews

  • On screens under 900px the header, search, panel, legend, and controls reflow instead of overflowing the desktop bar, and the verse panel fills the phone screen. The canvas takes one-finger drag, pan, and tap, and two-finger pinch-zoom.
  • Verse dots always show one theme word: the first content word, with stopwords stripped, rather than a label that appears only on hover.
  • The default minimum link strength is 5, down from 15, so the force layout starts with more of its springs already engaged.
  • Hovering a verse dot shows a short italic preview, about three or four words of the verse, above the reference.
  • The graph code was synced from the palingen-studios project (claude/bible-verse-graph-6t436x).
September 13, 2026 Live

Timeline chronology, ontology graph, and textual variants

  • The timeline gained an estimated-year axis, using Septuagint genealogical reckoning for the early chapters, and more interaction: click an arc for the two verses, and filter the arcs by book.
  • The ontology page gained a node-graph view beside the tree.
  • A textual-criticism page was added for a curated set of Masoretic, Septuagint, and Dead Sea Scrolls differences.
  • A static-build loading hang in background or hidden tabs was fixed. The load now yields with a timeout, which still fires when a tab is hidden, instead of waiting on an animation frame that the browser may not deliver.
